### Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website hero background for VeriID, an identity document OCR prototype. Generate a premium editorial photograph, landscape 16:9, 1920x1080 or wider. Scene: a modern professional workspace, close angled view of a slim open laptop, a person's natural hands holding a smartphone beside it, and a small out-of-focus plant at the far right. Only hands and forearms are visible. Composition: the laptop, phone and hands occupy the RIGHT 45% of frame; the entire LEFT 55% is quiet charcoal navy negative space fading from a dark wall into the desk, suitable for large white headline overlay. The laptop and phone screens show dim, out-of-focus neutral interface blocks only, no documents, no portraits, no personal data, no readable text. Real wood desk, realistic brushed aluminum laptop, subtle cobalt reflections and warm daylight highlights. Authentic high-end fintech product photography, shallow depth of field, understated, sophisticated, natural skin and material textures. The right-hand scene should remain visible under a dark translucent navy gradient. No illustrations, no cartoon, no 3D rendering aesthetic, no logos, no text, no watermarks, no fake ID cards, no floating graphic panels. This photo will be used behind an actual HTML interface containing the project's real redacted identity dataset samples.
